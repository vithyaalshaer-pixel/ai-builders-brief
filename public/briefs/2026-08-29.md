本周的科技公司财报电话会议再次提醒我们，软件和人工智能之间的关系至关重要。

软件为数据管理、工作流程中的业务逻辑维护、信息访问的管理和控制、数据保护等提供了保障。我们希望这些系统每次都能以相同的方式运行，绝不出错。

另一方面，智能体在这些系统内部运行，并使用这些数据来执行任务，就像人一样。但它们的规模远超以往，因此确定性控制比以往任何时候都更加重要。

最终结果是，软件和人工智能的采用率将齐头并进地提高。软件和代理将协同工作，并最终随着时间的推移，大幅扩大 IT TAM 的规模。

This week’s tech earnings calls were a critical reminder of how valuable the relationship is between software and AI.

Software provides the guardrails for how data is managed, how business logic in workflows are maintained, how you manage and govern access to information, how you protect data, and more. You want these systems to work the same way every time, without fail. 

Agents, on the other hand, work inside these systems and with this data to execute on tasks just like people would have. But at a scale far greater than people ever did, which is especially why the deterministic controls matter more than ever.  

And in fact many of the best ways to deploy agents will be directly within the software systems themselves, like from Salesforce, Box, Harvey, ServiceNow, and so on. These platforms can optimize agents for their workflows and domains, with evals and context that are unique to their vertical, as well as connect to other 
 agents headlessly.
