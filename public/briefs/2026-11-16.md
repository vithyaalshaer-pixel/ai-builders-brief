#### 中文正文

人工智能模型在所有编码、工具和计算机使用、数学以及其他与编码相关的任务方面，必然会不断进步。因此，能够使用软件完成工作或从零开始生成代码的智能体，其进步速度将远超其他所有知识领域的工作。

一方面，这可能意味着只有一小部分工作能够实现自动化。但更可能的情况是，越来越多的工作会根据智能体的优势进行编码调整。

从最近几个月发布的模型示例中就可以看出这一点，智能体可以生成代码，进而制作动画视频、游戏、产品3D模型等等。我们也知道，工具的使用使智能体能够操作许多人们已经在使用的软件。这有助于网络安全、生命科学、金融以及其他众多领域的发展。

我们传统的思维模式是，模型必须无所不知才能创造价值，但实际上，它们*真正*需要的是能够有效地使用或生成软件，并结合深厚的领域专业知识，才能在工作流程中做出正确的判断。

当拥有这种强大的组合时，几乎所有其他功能都将迎刃而解。这就是为什么知识型智能体的发展速度只会越来越快。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**AI models are inevitably going to keep getting better and better at all coding, too…**

AI models are inevitably going to keep getting better and better at all coding, tool and computer use, math, and other coding-related tasks. As a result, agents that can use software to do their work, or generate code from scratch, will improve at a faster rate than all other domains of knowledge work.

On one hand that could mean that only a small subset of work can get automated. But probably what happens is that more and more work just becomes coding-shaped to adapt to what agents are good at.

You can see it in the most recent examples of model releases in the past couple of months, where agents can generate code that becomes animated videos, games, 3D models for products, and so on. We also know that tool use gives agents the ability to work with much of the same software that people already work with. This helps in cyber, life sciences, finance, and a number of other domains.

Our mental model traditionally has been that the models have to be able to know everything about anything to generate value, but in reality they *really* have to know how to use or generate software effectively combined with deep domain expertise to make the right judgment calls inside of workflows. 

When you have that powerful combination, almost everything else gets unlocked. This is why the acceleration of agents in knowledge work is only going to increase from here.
