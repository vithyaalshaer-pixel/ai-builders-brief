为什么大多数企业人工智能项目都会失败？

1/ 沿用老一套的人工智能开发模式

CEO 挑选一位值得信赖的副手来领导一个中央人工智能团队。这位领导者从公司各部门召集了一批值得信赖的人员。

问题在于技能差距。

过去行之有效的团队结构、产品模式以及产品发布和学习方法并不适用于人工智能。过去 15 年的产品开发大多是渐进式改进。

而人工智能需要实验和创新。

2/ 对评估投入不足

大多数企业并不完全理解评估的重要性以及评估的有效性。（我在评论区有一系列关于评估的文章——欢迎查看）

3/ 从外部构建人工智能

中央人工智能团队将自己定位为平台团队，并开发出所有人都必须使用的工具。

这些产品往往与实际工作流程、工作环境和员工的判断脱节。

因此，员工往往不愿采用这些工具。但并没有带来实质性的生产力提升。

你应该这样做：

1/ 聘请成功打造过人工智能产品的领导者。你需要他们具备深厚的技术/产品功底，以及赢得那些被他们重塑工作岗位的人们的信任的能力。

2/ 将评估工作置于人工智能工作的首要位置。

3/ 将你最优秀的人工智能开发人员融入到你想要转型的职能部门中。与财务部门、销售部门和支持部门协同合作。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**Why most enterprise AI efforts fail.**

Why most enterprise AI efforts fail.

1/ Using old-school playbooks for AI
The CEO picks a trusted lieutenant to lead a central AI team. That leader pulls in a group of trusted people from across the company.

The problem here is the skill gap.

The team structure, product patterns, how you launch and learn that got you here do not work with AI. Much of product development over the last 15 yrs has been incremental improvements.

AI requires experimentation and invention.

2/ Under-investing in evals

Most enterprises don't quite understand the importance of this and what good looks like here. (I have a whole evals series in the comments - check it out)

3/ Building AI for the company from the outside

The central AI team declares itself a platform team and builds tools that everyone is supposed to adopt.

These products are often disconnected from the actual workflows, context and judgment of the people doing the work.

Begrudging adoption follows. But not meaningful productivity gains.

What you should do:

1/ Hire leaders who have done successfully built AI products. You need technical/product depth plus the ability to earn trust with the people whose jobs they’re helping reinvent.

2/ Make evals a first class citizen in your AI efforts

3/ Embed your best AI builders inside the functions you want to transform. Build * with * finance , build * with * sales, build * with * support.

**Hi Astra users.**

Hi Astra users. A reset and a quick update on quality issues that have been posted around.

Working with some of you, we have found and fixed the following issues:
- Some skills written for previous models were triggering too often or preventing the model from checking its work.
- An opt-in context management experiment that could cause early stops or replies to older messages. We've disabled it. Our rough estimate is that 4-5k users were affected by this experiment.
- We've also removed some badly configured engines that resulted in a measured quality degradation for a long tail of traffic flowing through them.

We’ve also made some more minor improvements and things should feel significantly better across the board. More consistent follow-through, better tracking of your latest message, and better checks on the work as it’s going through the motions.

The examples posted and all the users who worked directly with us were incredibly useful in helping fix things quickly. Always grateful for this incredible community.
