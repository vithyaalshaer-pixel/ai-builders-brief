#### 中文正文

提升评估能力的最佳方法 - 第三部分

我们来谈谈故障模式分类——这是你完成评估第一版后应该做的第一件事。

从生产环境的跟踪记录入手——研究最近 500 到 1000 次交互，找出故障。对故障进行聚类并命名。

要具体——“错误的答案”并不是一个有效的聚类名称。

具体的故障示例——例如：检索到错误的文档/检索到正确的文档但章节不相关/未能结合上下文进行推断/未能放弃答案而是编造内容/问题含糊不清，并做出错误的假设而不是提出澄清问题等等。

这些都是截然不同的问题。

一旦你能准确地命名故障，你就可以构建专门用于捕获故障的评估测试。

现在，你已经搭建了从评估到改进循环的桥梁。明天继续……

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**The best way to get good at evals - Part 3.**

The best way to get good at evals - Part 3. 

Let’s talk failure modes taxonomy - that’s the first thing you should build once you have v1 of your evals.

Start with your production traces - study the last 500 or 1,000 interactions and look at the failures. Cluster them and name the clusters.

Get specific - “bad answer” isn’t a useful cluster name. 

Specific failures - wrong document retrieved / right document but irrelevant section / failed to ground to context and hallucinated /  failed to punt on answer and instead made stuff up / question ambiguous  and made poor assumptions rather than asking clarifying questions etc.

Those are very different problems.

Once you can name the failure precisely, you can build eval tests specifically designed to catch it.

You now have the bridge from evals to an improvement flywheel. More tomorrow…

在人工智能时代，专家和通才之间的争论一直存在。目前来看，专家似乎占据了上风，而且这种趋势仍在持续。

人工智能让任何任务的入门都变得容易十倍，例如编程、法律工作、研究、财务分析等等。但是，如何正确判断如何引导人工智能完成正确的工作，如何纠正其错误方向，如何审查或测试输出结果，以及对“好”的标准有正确的认识，所有这些都需要高超的技能，具体技能水平取决于所处领域。

而且，人工智能很可能会加剧技能水平的差距，因为专家比以往任何时候都拥有更大的话语权。总而言之：不要放弃成为某个领域的专家。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**There tends to be a debate between being an expert or generalist in the era of AI.**

There tends to be a debate between being an expert or generalist in the era of AI. So far, the experts appear to have the upper hand, and that’s not slowing down.

AI makes it 10X easier to get started with any kind of task. Coding, legal work, research, financial analysis, and so on. But having the right judgment for how to direct the agent onto the right work, how to veer it to course correct it, being able to review or test the output, and having the right sense of what “good” looks like all requires a high degree of skill depending on the field.

That skill can be developed far faster due to AI for anyone interested, which is amazing for uplifting anyone in their career. But there’s no replacement to needing judgment and many of the core skills that go into most domains of work.
