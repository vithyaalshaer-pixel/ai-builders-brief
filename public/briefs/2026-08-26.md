#### 中文正文

人们并未充分认识到零数据限制 (ZDR) 对人工智能 (AI) 的蓬勃发展起到了至关重要的作用，因为它极大地简化了大多数公司在与子处理器处理数据时所面临的合规流程。否则，ZDR 的普及速度将会慢得多。

对于那些主要推动 AI 发展的应用型 AI 工具而言，它们通常与客户签订协议，只提供符合 ZDR 标准的模型，以确保客户无需对启用的每个模型进行审查。任何其他情况都需要经过漫长的例外流程。

而在企业内部，大多数公司都有内部治理要求，规定他们只能使用符合 ZDR 标准的模型，因为他们无法明确区分企业范围内在上下文窗口中输入的个人身份信息 (PII) 和其他敏感或机密数据。

如果整个行业都朝着这个方向发展，这一切都可能会改变，但由于个别模型仍坚持使用 ZDR，这些政策的改变可能需要数年时间。如果没有 ZDR，AI 的普及将停滞不前。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**It’s not fully appreciated that ZDR is responsible for a substantial amount of the…**

It’s not fully appreciated that ZDR is responsible for a substantial amount of the growth of AI, because it dramatically simplified the compliance process most companies deal with in handling data with subprocessors. The alternative path would have been a far slower adoption rate.

For the applied AI tools that are primarily responsible for driving AI growth, they generally have agreements in place with their customers to only offer models with ZDR to ensure that customers don’t have to inspect each model they turn on. Anything else is an exception process that takes far longer.

And then inside enterprises directly, most companies have internal governance requirements that say they can only use ZDR models because they have no way of explicitly separating PII and other sensitive or confidential data they put in the context window across their enterprise.

Now, all of this could change if the entire industry went this direction, but with one off models hold outs it would take years for these policies to change. Without ZDR, AI diffusion grinds to a halt.
