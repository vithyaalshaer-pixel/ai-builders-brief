#### 中文正文

你无法自动化你无法衡量的东西。这意味着评估是人工智能在企业中普及的关键环节之一。

我们可以通过软件测试确定性流程，但大多数企业目前没有有效的方法来了解其非确定性流程的运行情况，特别是智能体为他们所做的工作。

对于采用人工智能的企业而言，评估至关重要，因为如果你不了解智能体在当前环境中的运行情况，你就无法知道哪些有效、哪些无效、哪些发生了变化、哪些有所改进、哪些方面可以改进等等。所有变更、升级和部署都源于有效的评估。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**You can’t automate what you can’t measure.**

You can’t automate what you can’t measure. This means that evals are one of the gates to diffusion of AI in the enterprise. 

We can test our deterministic processes through software, but most enterprises have no useful way of understanding how their non-deterministic processes are working today. Specifically the work that agents are doing for them.

Evals are mission critical for enterprises adopting AI because you have no other way of knowing what’s working, what’s broken, what changed, what improved, what you can do more of, etc. if you don’t have a good sense of how agents work in your environment today. All changes, upgrades, and deployments are downstream from good evals.

Not only are we going to get vastly more domain specific evals over time for the labs and across the industry, but every enterprise will also need a clear sense of how agents are performing in their environment as well. Huge opportunity.
