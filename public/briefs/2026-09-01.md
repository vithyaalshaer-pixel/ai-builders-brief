#### 中文正文

有时人们无法直观地理解代币消费中的杰文斯悖论。而我们这些与企业合作的人每天都能亲眼目睹这一点。

基本上，企业有数不尽的任务，他们很希望能够实现自动化。但对于每个任务而言，自动化是否具有投资回报率取决于其成本（包括设置时间、变更管理等等）。

随着代币价格的下降（达到一定的功能阈值），企业可以负担得起将其应用于更多工作。这可能包括处理每个合约、读取每个日志、监控新的数据流以获取洞察、让后台代理执行工作流等等。

任何时候，只要我们能够降低代币成本，就会看到消费量不成比例地增长。即使代币价格下降 50%，也可能导致此类工作负载的代币消费量增长 5 倍。

这就是为什么持续降低人工智能成本至关重要，以及为什么这对所有市场参与者都有利。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**Sometimes people don’t have an intuitive sense of what Jevons paradox looks like fo…**

Sometimes people don’t have an intuitive sense of what Jevons paradox looks like for token consumption. Those of us working with enterprises get to see this first hand every day.

Basically enterprises have an unending stream of tasks that they’d love to be able to bring automation to. But for each individual task, it’s either ROI positive or not based on the cost of bringing automation to it (including the setup time, change management, and so on).

As tokens get cheaper (at a certain capability threshold), enterprises can afford bringing them to more of the work that they do. This could be processing every contract, reading every log, watching new streams of data for insights, having background agents execute workflows, and so on. 

Any time we can lower the cost of tokens, we will see a disproportionate increase in consumption. Even a 50% drop in token prices could result in a 5X increase in tokens for these kinds of workloads.

This is why it’s critical to keep bringing down the cost of AI, and why that’s good for all market participants.
