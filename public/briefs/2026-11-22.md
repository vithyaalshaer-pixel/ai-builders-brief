**This year we’ve had a clear jump in model capability where instead of telling agent…**

This year we’ve had a clear jump in model capability where instead of telling agents precisely what to do, it’s better to use these systems to solve problems you can’t easily define yourself. 

A year ago you’d need to very clearly tell agents the how to design and develop your product idea, or there was almost no chance of success.
