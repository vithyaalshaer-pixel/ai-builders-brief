#### 中文正文

我很好奇 X 是如何对抗 AI 垃圾信息的，所以我研究了它的开源算法。

它有一个名为 TweetSpamBot 的行为模型，可以分析多达 512 条近期账户操作，关注诸如发帖爆发、引用转发行为、发布时间以及正常浏览或操作间停留时间等信号。

该模型经过训练，可以识别以下模式：

→ 推文爆发

→ 引用推文垃圾信息

→ 内容放大

上述模式的高分可以用于账户挑战，但似乎并不会降低垃圾信息本身的排名。

我认为问题在于，该模型并没有读取实际的帖子内容。

所以，即使是技术高超的AI内容工厂也能整天发布垃圾内容：

1. 找到爆款帖子

2. 每次都用相同的模板转发推文：引子 → 数据 → 背景故事 → 要点

3. 每天在不相关的话题上重复10次以上

如果@X不想变成像LinkedIn那样，我认为它应该关注那些反复大量地将不相关的爆款帖子转换成相同模板的垃圾转发推文的账号。

目前，上述的防护措施太容易被绕过。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**I was curious how X is fighting AI slop, so I looked at its open-source algorithm.**

I was curious how X is fighting AI slop, so I looked at its open-source algorithm.

It has a behavioral model called TweetSpamBot that analyzes up to 512 recent account actions, looking at signals like posting bursts, quote-post behavior, timing, and normal browsing or dwell between actions.

The model is trained to recognize patterns such as:

→ TWEET_CREATE_BURST
→ QUOTE_TWEET_SPAMMER
→ CONTENT_AMPLIFIER

A high score on the above can feed into an account challenge but doesn't seem to downrank the slop posts themselves.

I think the gap here is that the model doesn’t read the actual post content.

So a sophisticated AI content mill can still post slop all day long:

1. Find viral posts
2. Quote tweet with the same template each time: hook → numbers → backstory → takeaway
3. Repeat 10+ times a day across unrelated topics

If @X doesn't want to become like LinkedIn, I think it should consider looking at accounts that repeatedly turn unrelated viral posts into the same templated quote tweet slop at high volume.

Right now the guardrails above are too easy to bypass.
