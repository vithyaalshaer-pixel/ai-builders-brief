为成千上万代表我们行事的智能代理而设计的身份、权限和安全系统。

为智能代理和人类用户或两者协同工作而设计的界面。

如果您正在寻找创业灵感，请逐层审视计算栈，并思考：当主要用户是智能代理时，哪些方面需要改变？

There are massive startup opportunities hiding in plain sight : every layer of the computing stack will need to be rebuilt for agents.

For the last few years, most of the focus has been on making enterprise software agent-ready: APIs, connectors, MCPs.

Now personal agents are forcing the same conversation for consumer products - e-commerce is an early example.

This is just the tip of the iceberg.

Now think about the rest of the stack:

Desktop and mobile OSes where agents are first-class users

Interfaces designed for agent and human users or both working together.
