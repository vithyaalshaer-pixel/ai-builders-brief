#### 中文正文

无论你是企业内部的人工智能开发者，还是企业人工智能的构建者，这篇文章都值得一读。

人工智能带来的生产力提升将远超你的想象，因为如果你从根本上改变工作流程来支持智能体，那么在人工智能前沿领域所能取得的成就将非常显著。然而，挑战在于，由于其复杂性，大多数人不会或无法自然而然地做到这一点。

因此，智能体的自动化程度很大程度上取决于如何将智能体集成到用户几乎不会注意到或关心的工作流程中：

“对其他人来说，工作必须在不改变他们现有工作方式的情况下完成，这意味着人工智能将在后台运行。为什么人工智能需要人为干预呢？只需找出重复性最高的流程，并在员工已经习惯的现有系统中构建智能体即可。”

这才是目前大多数希望从人工智能中获得显著收益的企业真正需要努力的方向。与其说是“让千朵花盛开”，不如说是“挑选企业中最具杠杆效应的10件事，并应用自动化技术”。这里没有真正的捷径。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**Great post both if you’re driving AI in an enterprise or building for an enterprise.**

Great post both if you’re driving AI in an enterprise or building for an enterprise. 

AI productivity gains are going to wildly vary - much wider than you think - because what you can do at the frontier is so significant if you fundamentally change your workflows to support agents. The challenge is that most people won’t or can’t naturally do this because of the complexity it entails.

Thus, a large amount of automation with agents is going to come from just getting agents wired up into a workflow that the user never ends up noticing or caring about:

“For everybody else, the work has to get done without them changing how they work, which means the AI goes into the background. Why do you need AI to be prompted by a human anyway? Just figure out what the most repetitive processes are, build agents in your existing systems of record that employees are used to”

This is the real work ahead for most enterprises right now that want to get significant gains from AI. A lot less “let a thousand flowers bloom” and more “pick off the 10 highest leverage things in the enterprise and apply automation to them”. There’s no real shortcut here.
