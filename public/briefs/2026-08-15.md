#### 中文正文

Grok Bot 的发布真是太棒了……用户体验、设计、引导流程都无可挑剔！

但我一直在纠结这个问题：用户（个人和企业用户）究竟是想要一个掌握所有上下文信息的超级智能体，还是想要多个子智能体分别负责不同的任务？

如果我们把智能体看作人，那么 Grok Bot 的设计就非常合理。它允许我们根据用户的具体需求，将工具、上下文和结果进行分类。用户可以根据自己的目标与相应的智能体进行沟通。

但是，如果把智能体看作 Jarvis，那么用户就需要一个能够完成任务的主智能体。它可以创建并协调完成任务所需的所有智能体（甚至可以说包括沟通和上下文）。但界面是一对一的，而不是一对多的。

我猜测，随着时间的推移，最终会倾向于使用一个主智能体，它可以作为协调者，读取并理解用户创建的所有智能体。但一开始就比较狭窄，因为现在大多数公司都是这么做的。

附注：我理解公司如今选择任务/单一结果的核心工程限制（上下文窗口、工具使用、简洁性、成本等）。我主要是从设计/产品角度来考虑这个问题。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**Grok Bot is a fantastic release..**

Grok Bot is a fantastic release.. the UX, the design, the onboarding :chefs-kiss:

But I go back and forth on this a lot: Will users (personal and business) want one super agent that holds all the context or sub agents that are designated to do a slice of tasks?

If we treat agents as humans, then the Grok Bot design makes a ton of sense. It allows us to compartmentalize tools, context, outcomes for that person’s division. You reach out to that bot for that specific goal that you have in mind. 

But, if you treat agents as Jarvis, you want a master agent that just gets the job done. It might create and coordinate all the bots (and dare I say the communication/context) that might be needed to do the task. But the interface is 1:1 vs. 1:many. 

My guess is it’ll veer towards the one master agent over time who can then act as an orchestrator who reads / understands the bots you create. But starts narrow as most of the companies are doing it now. 

PS: I understand the core engineering constraints (context windows, tool use, simplicity, cost etc) why companies are choosing task/singular outcomes today. Primarily thinking this from a design / product side.

</details>
