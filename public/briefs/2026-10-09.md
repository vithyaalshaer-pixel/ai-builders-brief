如今，一位雄心勃勃的软件工程师应该做什么？

十年前，成为一名优秀的软件工程师会给你带来一种独特的优势：一项几乎可以应用于任何行业的稀缺技能。

你可以创建一家支付公司，改进医院的运营方式，或者创造一种全新的沟通方式。同样的底层能力为你打开了通往截然不同行业的大门。

软件正在席卷全球，掌握软件开发技能几乎让你在任何领域都占据一席之地。

“精通软件工程”是一条非常通用的建议。你可以先掌握这项技能，然后再去探索它的应用领域。

人工智能正在让开发出可用软件的能力变得更加普及。几年前无法开发产品的人，现在却能取得令人惊讶的成就。经验丰富的工程师可以尝试以前需要团队协作才能完成的项目。

优秀的工程技术仍然稀缺。但是，“我会开发”这个理由，对于你个人的优势而言，已经显得有些苍白无力了。

在我看来，有三条路可走。

1. 投身于软件开发的核心挑战

帮助打造让软件工程更易于上手的技术。

这可能意味着人工智能研究，或是开发实现新功能所需的基础设施、系统和工具。找到那些技术能力仍然严重受限的问题，以及解决这些问题能够拓展他人能力的领域。

2. 寻找世界级的领域专家并组建团队

在某个行业深耕十五年的专家可能深谙哪些地方容易出现代价高昂的错误，之前的修复尝试为何失败，以及谁会购买解决方案。这些知识很难从外部获得。

在SPC，我们看到越来越多的人成为极具潜力的创始人，尽管他们之前并没有太多软件工程经验。他们拥有深厚的领域知识和对市场需求的敏锐洞察力。

这些人值得结识。一位优秀的工程合作伙伴能够看到他们未曾考虑过的可能性，并帮助他们构建出两人独自都无法想象的产品。

3. 编写基础代码……通过管理一群智能体

这是我最不感兴趣的方向：在公司里做着大致相同的工作，只不过现在你要管理的是编码智能体。

这类工作确实有价值。但如果你的目标仅仅是更快地完成同样的待办事项，那你就错失良机了。

我仍然会努力提升自己的工程技能。然后我会认真思考如何将这项技能运用到更广阔的领域：是需要推进的技术前沿，还是对某个值得解决的问题的深入理解。

利用你拥有的构建能力，承担更重要的责任。

**What should an ambitious software engineer do today?**

What should an ambitious software engineer do today?

Ten years ago, being a great software engineer gave you an unusual advantage: a rare skill you could apply to almost any industry.

You could build a payments company, improve how hospitals operated, or create a new way for people to communicate. The same underlying ability opened doors into wildly different businesses.

Software was eating the world, and knowing how to build it gave you a seat at the head of almost every table.

“Get really good at software engineering” was remarkably versatile advice. You could acquire the skill first and figure out where to apply it later.

AI is making the ability to produce working software much more widely available. People who couldn’t have built a product a few years ago can now get surprisingly far. Experienced engineers can attempt projects that previously required a team.

Great engineering is still rare. But “I can build it” is becoming a weaker answer to why you, specifically, have an advantage.

The way I see it, there are three paths.

1. Go work on the thing that’s eating software

Help create the technology that is making software engineering more accessible.

That could mean AI research, or the infrastructure, systems, and tools needed to make new capabilities useful. Find problems where technical ability remains a serious constraint, and where solving them expands what other people can do.

2. Find a world-class domain expert and team up

Someone who has spent fifteen years in an industry may know where the expensive mistakes happen, why previous attempts to fix them failed, and who would buy a solution. That knowledge is hard to acquire from the outside.

At SPC, we’re seeing more people become promising founders without much prior software engineering experience. They bring deep domain knowledge and strong instincts about what needs to exist.

These are people worth getting to know. A great engineering partner can see possibilities they haven’t considered and help build something neither person could have imagined alone.

3. Write vanilla code… by shepherding a bunch of agents

This is the direction I find least compelling: doing roughly the same work inside a company, except now you supervise coding agents.

There will be valuable jobs like this. But if your ambition extends only to completing the same backlog faster, you’re underusing the moment.

I’d still get very good at engineering. Then I’d be deliberate about what that ability sits next to: a technical frontier that needs advancing, or deep knowledge of a problem worth solving.

Use the fact that you can build more to take responsibility for something bigger.
