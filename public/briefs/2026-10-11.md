#### 中文正文

开放权重模型并非智能单位价格下降的始作俑者，它们只是加速了这一趋势的到来。

过去几年，以下几个因素推动了价格下降：

1/ 模型构建者将他们最好的模型精简为更小的模型，从而降低推理成本

2/ 基础设施效率的提升

3/ 模型提供商之间的竞争

几乎所有模型公司都遵循这一模式：中等规模模型的第 x 版智能相当于大型模型的第 x-1 版智能。同样的智能，更低的价格。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**Open-weight models did not start the drop in price per unit of intelligence.**

Open-weight models did not start the drop in price per unit of intelligence. They are a tailwind on a trend that was already underway.

For the last few years, a few factors have driven price drops:

1/ Model builders distilling their best models into smaller ones, with cheaper inference

2/ Infra efficiency

3/ Competition between model providers

You can see this pattern with every model company: Version x of the mid-size model = intelligence of version x-1 of the large model.  Same intelligence for less $.

</details>
