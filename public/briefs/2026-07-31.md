#### 中文正文

Dwarkesh 的帖子引人深思。总的来说，随着人工智能能力的提升，我们应该预期推理任务会优先用于最具经济价值的任务，而其他任务则会因成本过高而被淘汰。

理论上，在资源极其稀缺的环境下，推理成本会飙升，因为这些任务的价值将远高于现在。

但我认为实际情况并非如此，原因很简单，市场力量会竞相争夺推理需求，从而持续压低价格，直到产能跟上需求为止。

有太多的模型提供商和基础设施厂商都希望获得这些工作负载，因此不太可能出现这里所设想的情况。让我们拭目以待！

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**Thought provoking post by Dwarkesh.**

Thought provoking post by Dwarkesh. In general - as AI gets more powerful - we should expect on the margin that inference goes toward the most economically useful task, and everything else gets priced out. 

This in theory in a very scarce environment would cause the cost of inference to skyrocket because those tasks would be far more valuable than today. 

But I don’t think it plays out in the way that’s laid out just simply due to market forces competing for inference demand as a way of continuing to drive down prices until capacity can catch up.

There are too many model providers and infra players that want these workloads to cause the effect that’s proposed here. But will see!
