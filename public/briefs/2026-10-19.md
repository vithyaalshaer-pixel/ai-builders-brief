#### 中文正文

那些预言软件工程终结的人最大的误判在于，他们以为世界只需要一定量的软件，而将自动化引入该领域就能减少对劳动力的需求。

事实恰恰相反，现在几乎所有东西都在向软件转化。由于智能体可以使用工具、与计算机交互、即时编写软件，并像人类一样处理数据（但速度更快），我们现在可以用“软件”来解决以前根本无法数字化解决的问题。

这导致工程在经济中扮演着越来越重要的角色，因为我们现在可以设计比以往多得多的系统和流程。

如果你是一家银行，想要开展新的交易方式，工程师通常是你的首选。如果你从事生命科学，你会预期越来越多的研究和实验室工作将以工程为中心。如果你从事制造业，你正在努力实现供应链的自动化，所有这些都属于工程范畴。越来越多的工作都以软件问题的形式呈现。

是的，未来我们不会再手动编写代码了。但几乎一切都将以代码的形式呈现，这意味着那些懂代码的人将处于非常有利的地位。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**The biggest misread of anyone predicting the end of software engineering was imagin…**

The biggest misread of anyone predicting the end of software engineering was imagining that the world only needed a certain amount of software, and bringing automation to the field would reduce the need for labor. 

Exactly the opposite is happening where essentially most things are actually turning into software. Because agents can use tools, work with computers, write software on the fly, and process data like humans (but far faster), we can now throw “software” at problems that never could reasonably be digitized before.

What that leads to is the role of engineering taking on greater importance in the economy, because we can now engineer far more systems and processes than were possible before.

If you’re a bank trying to do new forms of trading, engineers are often your first bet. If you are in life sciences, you expect more and more research and lab work to be engineering centric. If you’re in manufacturing, you’re trying to automate more of the supply chain, all that is engineering. More and more work is shaped like software problems.

Yes we’re not going to be coding manually in the future. But almost everything will be code, which means those that understand it are in a fantastic position.
