人工智能就业前景黯淡的说法并未像某些人预测的那样出现。我接触过的大部分企业——涵盖各个行业——仍在招聘，只是招聘的岗位类型有所调整。

他们发现人工智能让他们能够做更多的事情，杰文斯悖论也正在上演。他们招聘工程师来解决以前无法解决的问题。他们招聘销售人员，因为借助人工智能，他们现在可以更深入地了解客户关系。他们招聘内部的现场工程师来帮助他们部署人工智能。等等。

任何仅仅为了降低成本而使用人工智能的企业，最终都会被那些利用人工智能更好地服务客户并推动业务突破的公司所超越。

这种趋势会在未来发生改变吗？当然会。但就目前而言，这似乎就是我们正走在的发展道路上。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**The negative AI jobs outcome just continues to not be happening as some predicted.**

The negative AI jobs outcome just continues to not be happening as some predicted. A large portion of enterprises I talk with -across industries- are still hiring, just with a tilt in the kind of roles they’re going after.

They’re finding that AI is letting them do more, and Jevons paradox is actually playing out. They’re hiring engineers to go after problems they couldn’t tackle before. They’re hiring in sales because they can go deeper in client relationships now with the help of AI. They’re hiring in internal FDEs to help them deploy AI. And so on.

Anyone using AI merely to cut costs eventually just gets outcompeted by companies that use AI to better serve their customers and drive more breakthroughs in their business. 

Could this trend change at some point? Sure. But for now this appears to be the trajectory we’re on.
