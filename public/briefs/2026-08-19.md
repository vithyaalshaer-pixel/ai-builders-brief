**The best way to get good at evals is to take a workflow you know really well and fi…**

The best way to get good at evals is to  take a workflow you know really well and figure out how to make its quality measurable.

Study the actual traces - the sequence of prompts typical users have, what good responses would look like at each step and for the end to end outcome. 

Study where your product fails and create traces that capture them - messy tool call responses, missing context etc.

Once you have a good eval, think about how you would make it easy to run repeatedly and automatically. 

Once you have that, think about how you will ensure your eval continues to mirror live traffic as your users patterns evolve.

#### 中文正文

我最核心的观点是，品牌营销将成为未来公司最重要的差异化因素和最宝贵的资产之一。

而且，我说的不是发布视频或者为了提升估值而把钱砸在“美观”上。在吸引用户注意力之前，你仍然需要留住用户。

我指的是认真思考公司的价值观，并理解市场氛围。在坚持长期目标的同时，也要思考如何在短期内展现公司形象。而且要每周都这样做。

尤其如果你认为经纪人将成为大多数产品的主要用户，那么为了吸引注意力极易分散的受众（也就是人类），你需要在公司形象的呈现方式上始终保持独特性。

这些人之前大多担任二线职位，多次与首席营销官（CMO）职位失之交臂。我认为这种情况在未来十年内将会彻底改变，届时你会聘请他们担任联合创始人，或者至少让他们在公司决策中占据重要地位。

通常来说，以设计为中心的CEO往往具备这种特质，但这种人才非常稀少，你需要学习如何招聘这类人才。就像优秀的营销人员一样，这类人才极其罕见，所以一定要好好珍惜！

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**My hottest take is that brand marketing is going to be THE major differentiator and…**

My hottest take is that brand marketing is going to be THE major differentiator and one of the most prized assets for a company going forward. 

And, I’m not talking about launch videos or pouring money primarily for “aesthetics” simply to pump the valuation. You still need retention before attention. 

I’m talking about thinking through what a company stands for and understanding the “vibe” of what’s happening in the market. Being relentless on the long term goal while also thinking through how to showcase the company in the short term. And doing it every week. 

Especially if you think agents become the primary user for most products, then to get the attention of a rapidly attention deficit audience (aka humans) you’ll need to stand out consistently in how you present the company. 

These folks primary have been given tier 2 roles and passed up for CMOs many many times. I think this will finally invert in the next 10 years where you’ll hire these folks as cofounders or at least give them a big seat at the table. 

Normally the design centric CEO tends to be this, but those are rare and you’ll need to learn how to hire for this role. Just like a good product marketer, these are exceptionally rare so you have one hold them close!!

</details>
