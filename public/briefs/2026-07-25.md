未来，专家们将精进技艺，能力大幅提升。因为他们能够以有效的方式运用这些工具，从而产出高质量的成果，在智能体偏离正轨时将其引导回正轨，并将工作成果转化为有用的产品。

**The best way to think about AI is as a force multiplier for the fields you already…**

The best way to think about AI is as a force multiplier for the fields you already know about (or for the rate at which you want to learn about a new one). The 3rd category -no existing judgment, no interest in developing it- will basically produce slop and won’t drive that much economically productive activity. 

What will happen is that the experts will get better and better at their craft and be able to do so much more. Because they can wield these tools in ways that actually drive high quality output, veer agents in the right direction when they go off, and actually incorporate the work into something useful.

The expert engineer with agents will do far more productive work, precisely because they know how to steer the agent properly. The designer will product far better outcomes using AI than someone without an eye for design. And so on.

As a result of this dynamic, specialization will continue to be important, if not even more so as the tools get more powerful. Because the expectation of the market gets to be much higher. Getting good at any craft will continue to be necessary in the future.
