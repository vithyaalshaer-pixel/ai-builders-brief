这触及了人工智能领域核心辩论之一的本质。

如果一个封闭的协议栈始终遥遥领先于其他技术，那么在美国，垂直整合和把关策略就能奏效。因为你始终可以控制谁能获得最先进的技术，而且这些技术需求量足够大，最终总是对你有利。

然而，如果开放权重人工智能能够紧随前沿智能之后，那么情况就完全相反了。通过高度监管，你仍然可以掌控前沿市场，但绝大多数代币将流向另一个协议栈。这个协议栈最终将包含模型及其底层硬件。而这个协议栈将由其他人控制并从中获利。

你对开放权重人工智能能够与前沿智能保持多近的距离的看法——以及你认为有多少代币会流向前沿智能，又有多少代币会流向其他智能——将决定你对如何监管和控制人工智能的看法。

<details><summary>英文原文</summary>

**This gets to the core of one of the central debates in AI.**

This gets to the core of one of the central debates in AI. 

If a closed stack is always perpetually at the frontier by a wide margin, then being vertically integrated, and gate keeping in the US can work. Because you always have control over who gets access to the best technology, and it will be in high enough demand that it always favors you.

If, however, open weights AI can remain a close second to frontier intelligence, then the equation reverses. With a highly regulated approach, you’ll own the frontier market still, but the vast majority of tokens used will go to an alternative stack. That stack will include the model and the underlying hardware that runs it, in the limit. And that stack will be controlled and monetized by someone else. 

Depending on your belief on how close to the frontier open weights can remain -and similarly what percentage of tokens will go through the frontier vs. everything else- your opinion will be different on how to regulate and control AI.
